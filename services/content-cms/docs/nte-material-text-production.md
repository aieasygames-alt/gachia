# NTE existing material text production slice

The independent production path now accepts exactly one existing NTE material record, limited to existing `name` and `nameEn` text leaves. Its explicit scope is `nte-independent-existing-material-text-v1`. The character scope remains separately readable for historical receipts.

The compiler compares B with the complete 115-source published A. Other source overlays preserve A; they do not authorize edits to other games. Record identity, membership/order, rarity, type, source/provenance, media and relationships cannot change through this contract. Both A and B require complete NTE material dependencies.

A material build derives its 174 required collection/detail consumers from source. Sealing derives the same assertions for both candidate and compatible recovery, including names, collection and reverse character links, canonicals and existing robots policy. The Japanese collection remains noindex and Japanese details remain indexable. Router acceptance binds public raw JSON, all three search projections, platform data and character material readers, including both canonical Zero variants. Platform aggregates contain no material names; their unchanged values are checked.

The desktop entry still requires a real approved CMS record, a verified installed CMS ancestor, exact executor CI, complete published history, baseline deployment identity and immutable candidate/recovery previews. Only `gachia-game-nte` may receive traffic changes. Other games' dependency and consumer gaps are handled in their own slices.

First production pilot: material `collectors-drama-core`, `nameEn`: `Collector's Drama Core` → `Collector’s Drama Core`. This is punctuation normalization with no change in game facts. Operational evidence lives in `reports/nte-material-r130-2026-11-18/`; the final completion and source index determine whether it has actually been deployed.
